## Goal
After selecting a world, players choose which characters to load into their play session. This is the "character roster selection" that happens before starting the actual gameplay experience.

## Context
Once a player has selected a world (R3-0.7), they need to choose which characters will be active in their session. This is different from character creation - players are selecting from **published characters** created by others, not building their own. Think party selection in RPGs or choosing which NPCs to interact with.

## Acceptance Criteria

- ✅ Extend R3-1 schema for character discovery:
  - `published_characters` table: `id`, `world_id`, `character_name`, `creator_user_id`, `description`, `tags`, `character_data`, `published_at`, `featured`, `is_active`
