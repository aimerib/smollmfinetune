## Implementation Notes

The key insight was recognizing that `page_dataset_preview()` and `page_dataset_explorer_v2()` represented a **design problem** rather than just a refactoring opportunity. Instead of extracting them separately, we:

1. **Analyzed user workflows** and identified the primary use case (interactive generation)
2. **Simplified the interface** by removing unnecessary complexity 
3. **Unified the experience** into a single coherent "Dataset Studio"
4. **Used TDD** to ensure the new design met user needs

This approach delivered far more value than a simple extraction would have provided.
